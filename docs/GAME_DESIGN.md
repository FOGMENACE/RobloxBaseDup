# Game Design

## Vision

A polished bowling-ball progression game with satisfying movement,
a welcoming lobby, distinctive ball unlocks, and rewarding gameplay.

## Design Priorities

- Responsive, enjoyable movement
- Clear progression and rewards
- A comfortable, visually cohesive presentation
- Meaningful ball unlocks
- Accessible onboarding and navigation
- Reliable multiplayer gameplay

## Open Design Questions

- Final core gameplay loop
- Exact rebirth requirements and rewards
- Final progression balance
- Lobby layout and interaction points
- Audio direction and UI styling

Do not invent final design decisions without reviewing existing
prompts and implementation.
